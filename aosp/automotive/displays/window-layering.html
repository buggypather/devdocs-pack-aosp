Automotive window layering

In Automotive device configurations where safety-critical components are rendered in the same area of the screen as Android Automotive, it's imperative that Android layers are behind regulatory layers. Although it's possible to have transparent regions in Android, they aren't officially supported and can't be relied upon. Automotive device implementations MUST NOT layer Android Automotive OS on top of any safety relevant or regulatory components.

Figure 1. Window layers.

Devices can render non-critical components underneath Android. However, we recommended you use Android as the base layer when multiple systems render in the same space.
